# Luciole colours

Noctalia makes one palette and gives it to the whole desktop. Luciole follows it, so the file manager matches the bar and the panels.

## What follows the palette

- The bar, the panels and the launcher.
- GTK and Qt applications, and btop.
- The window frames and the root menu.
- Cava, Geany and PyRadio.
- Luciole.

## Change the colours

1. Change the wallpaper, or let the slideshow do it. The palette follows the wallpaper.
2. Or pick another colour scheme in the Noctalia settings, and switch between dark and light there.
3. Wait a few seconds. Luciole changes by itself, without a restart.

## Luciole stays in the old colours

1. Change the wallpaper once.
2. Still the old colours? Open the [technical notes](https://github.com/musqz/mabox-noctalia-ce/blob/main/docs/technical/luciole.md#colours).
